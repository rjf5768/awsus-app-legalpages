Privacy Policy

This policy explains how FormCue collects, uses, shares, retains, and protects information when you use the app.

Effective and last updated: October 6, 2026

Who operates FormCue

FormCue is operated by Rong Feng, an individual developer. Questions and privacy requests can be sent to rongf98@outlook.com.

Information we collect

Account and profile

We collect your username, email address, app and authentication identifiers, profile photo, bio, sport and skill information, favorite sports locations, account timestamps, and settings. Amazon Cognito handles passwords; FormCue does not store your password in its app database.

Content and social activity

We collect posts, comments, likes, follows, uploaded photos and videos, private messages, block relationships, and reports you submit. Content you share publicly can be viewed by other users. Messages are visible to conversation participants and are processed by automated safety systems.

Location

Location is hidden by default. If you choose discovery by location, we collect the exact, approximate, or custom location you select and use it to find nearby players. You can hide location again in Settings, which removes the active discovery record.

AI Coach

When you use AI Coach, we collect the sports video you choose, extracted frames, sport and action selections, requested feedback language, analysis status, model output, and technical usage information.

Safety and operations

We collect report reasons and details, target content identifiers and previews, support communications, security events, request counts, error information, and limited operational logs.

How we use information

  • Authenticate accounts and provide profiles, feeds, search, nearby-player discovery, chat, and media.
  • Generate AI Coach feedback and enforce the two-use launch allowance.
  • Screen user content, investigate reports, enforce blocks and community rules, prevent abuse, and secure the service.
  • Respond to support, privacy, and account-deletion requests.
  • Monitor reliability, control costs, diagnose failures, and maintain backups.

FormCue does not sell personal information, run third-party advertising, or use cross-app tracking.

Automated content safety

Text in profiles, posts, comments, and messages, and images selected for profiles or image posts, may be sent to OpenAI's moderation service before publication or delivery. Content identified as potentially violating the community rules may be rejected. Post videos are not automatically classified by this system; users can report them for review.

Service providers and disclosure

  • Amazon Web Services: Cognito authentication, AppSync APIs, DynamoDB data, S3 media, Lambda processing, Step Functions workflows, logs, security, and backups.
  • OpenAI: AI Coach frame analysis and automated text/image safety screening.
  • Apple and Expo: app distribution, TestFlight, build/submission services, and related platform operations.

We may also disclose information when required by law, to investigate abuse, to protect users or the service, or with your direction. Service providers process information only to provide their contracted functions.

Retention and deletion

  • Profiles and user content remain while the account or content is active, unless you delete them sooner.
  • AI Coach source videos expire after 7 days and extracted frames after 1 day. Analysis results remain until you delete them or delete your account.
  • Application Lambda logs are retained for 14 days.
  • Noncurrent S3 media versions expire within 30 days. DynamoDB point-in-time recovery may retain restorable changes for up to 35 days.
  • Moderation reports may be retained for up to 2 years. When an account is deleted, report records may be anonymized and retained to prevent abuse and document safety decisions.
  • Support records may be retained for up to 2 years unless a longer period is legally required.

Deleting an account removes active app-owned profile, content, social, location, chat, and AI records. Backup copies age out under the schedules above and are not restored except for disaster recovery.

Your choices

You can edit your profile, hide location, delete your posts and comments, delete AI analyses, block users, submit reports, and delete your account in Profile > Settings > Delete account. You can also email us to request access, correction, or deletion where applicable.

Children

FormCue is not directed to children under 13, and users under 13 may not create an account. If you believe a child under 13 has provided information, contact us so we can investigate and delete it.

Security and international processing

We use authenticated APIs, least-privilege cloud permissions, encrypted transport, private media storage, rate limits, monitoring, and backups. No service can guarantee absolute security. Information may be processed in the United States and other locations where our providers operate.

Policy changes

We may update this policy as FormCue changes. We will update the date above and provide additional notice in the app when a change materially affects how information is handled.
