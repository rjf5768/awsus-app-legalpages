Include your FormCue username, device type, app version, what you expected, and what happened. Do not send your password, confirmation code, or AWS/OpenAI credentials.

Report abuse

Use the in-app report action on the relevant post, comment, profile, or message so the report contains the correct identifiers. Block the account if you do not want to see or receive content from that user. For urgent safety or privacy concerns, also email support.

Target response times are within 24 hours for safety and privacy reports and within 72 hours for other support requests during launch.

Common questions

How do I delete my account?

Open Profile, tap Settings, then Delete account. Confirm the deletion. This removes the active account and app-owned data; bounded backup and safety retention is described in the Privacy Policy.

How do I hide my location?

Open Profile, Settings, Location privacy, choose Hidden, and save. FormCue then removes your active nearby-player location record.

Why was my content rejected?

FormCue checks user content for material that may violate the community rules. Revise the content and try again. Email support if you believe the decision was incorrect.

How many AI Coach analyses can I use?

Each account currently receives two lifetime AI Coach uses. For an access question, email support from the address associated with your account.

How do I report a technical problem?

Email support with the screen name, approximate time, device and iOS version, and steps that reproduce the issue. A screenshot is helpful when it does not contain private information.
